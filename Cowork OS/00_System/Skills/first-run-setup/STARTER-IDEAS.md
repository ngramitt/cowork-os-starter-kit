# Starter Workstation Ideas

Last updated: 2026-09-28 (living document; Cowork OS Starter Kit v1.0)

A menu for first-run setup. Pick **two or three** you'll use this month; more come naturally later. When a topic comes up twice and has nowhere to go, that's the signal to start a workstation for it.

Each idea has a sample routing condition (rewrite it in your own words, naming your real projects, people and places) and the typed subfolders it usually grows into.

## Work and money

| Workstation | Route here when I... | Usually grows |
|---|---|---|
| **Job Search** | ...am looking for a new role: applications, résumé versions, interview prep, recruiters, offers and negotiation | `Applications/`, `Interview Prep/`, `Tracking/` |
| **Side Business** | ...am working on my side project or small business: ideas, customers, pricing, marketing, numbers | `Strategy and Analysis/`, `Customers/`, `Tracking/` |
| **Money** | ...am working on budgeting, saving, investing, retirement accounts or big purchases | `Planning/`, `Statements/` (summaries, not account numbers) |
| **Taxes** | ...am preparing taxes: documents to gather, deductions, questions for my accountant | `Documents-Checklist.md`, `Correspondence/` |
| **Learning** | ...am learning a skill or taking a course: notes, practice plans, projects | `Notes/`, `Practice/` |

## Life admin

| Workstation | Route here when I... | Usually grows |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | ...am dealing with my home: repairs, contractors, moving, furniture, the lease or mortgage | `Projects/`, `Contacts and Quotes/` |
| **Admin and Paperwork** | ...am handling insurance, bills, subscriptions, disputes or anything with a form and a deadline | `Correspondence/`, `Evidence/`, `Tracking/` |
| **Travel** | ...am planning a trip: dates, bookings, itineraries, packing, budgets | one folder per trip |
| **Events** | ...am planning an event: a wedding, a party, a reunion, guests, vendors and the budget | `Vendors/`, `Guests/`, `Timeline.md` |

## Health and people

| Workstation | Route here when I... | Usually grows |
|---|---|---|
| **Health and Fitness** | ...am working on training plans, races, nutrition habits, sleep, or questions for my doctor | `Training/`, `Appointments/` |
| **Family and Friends** | ...am planning gifts, birthdays, visits, or helping someone I care about | `Gift Ideas/`, `Occasions/` |
| **Reflection** | ...want to think something through: decisions, goals, a journal, a weekly review | `Reviews/`, a living `Reflection Space.md` |

## Making things

| Workstation | Route here when I... | Usually grows |
|---|---|---|
| **Creative Projects** *(a nested parent)* | ...am making something: writing, photography, music, video. Each medium becomes its own area, e.g. `Creative Projects → Photography` | one sub-workstation per medium |
| **Writing** | ...am writing: essays, a newsletter, fiction, drafts and ideas | `Drafts/`, `Ideas/`, `Published/` |
| **Reading and Notes** | ...want to capture what I'm reading: books, articles, highlights and takeaways | `Books/`, `Articles/` |

## A note on work projects

If your employer has rules about AI tools or where work files may live, follow them. This folder sits on your own computer, and whatever AI tool you connect reads the files it needs. Many people keep this workspace for personal life and use their company's approved tools for work.
